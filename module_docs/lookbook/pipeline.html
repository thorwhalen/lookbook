lookbook.pipeline

Pipeline orchestrator.

A Pipeline is a list of scorers (and, later, filters/embedders) plus a final selector. Running it:

  1. Topologically orders the scorers by their requires declarations.

  2. Walks them in cost-tier order, writing results to the manifest.

  3. Hands the surviving candidates plus the read-only manifest to the selector.

For Phase 0 the orchestration is deliberately simple — a hand-rolled topo walk. Phase 1+ will swap in meshed.DAG for richer dependency graphs and caching.
